The September 30, 2026 filing deadline applies to all UAE businesses with a financial year ending December 31, 2025. If your business registered for Corporate Tax but has not yet filed its return, this deadline applies to you.

Preparing and filing a Corporate Tax return correctly takes time — records must be reviewed, taxable income calculated, and the return confirmed before submission.
