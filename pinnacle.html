Yes — that's the Pinnacle difference. Legal advisory is built into your retainer, not billed as an add-on. Legal leadership reviews up to four commercial agreements annually — supplier contracts, partnership agreements, service contracts, and more — alongside your financial advisory, so both sides of every decision are covered by one team.

Pinnacle is designed for established UAE businesses — typically 7+ years of operation — where risks are more complex: businesses with multiple entities or group structures, companies in active investor or board relationships, businesses with complex commercial contracts or transfer pricing exposure, and family businesses and holding structures.

IHG Shield covers growing businesses with bookkeeping, external audit, VAT, CT, and FTA support. IHG Pinnacle is the senior tier: it upgrades reporting to board-based analysis, deepens the audit engagement with a detailed management letter, adds transfer pricing awareness, and — uniquely — builds legal advisory into the retainer with four commercial agreements reviewed per year.

Pinnacle clients can engage IHG for court accounting expert reports, arbitration advisory, company formation and structuring, bankruptcy and financial restructuring, trust and family foundation formation, and investment proposals — senior capacity on call, scoped and priced per engagement.
