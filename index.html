FAQs

Common questions about IHG.

Everything you need to know about our packages and UAE compliance.

An IHG annual retainer is a fixed-fee engagement covering all the compliance and advisory services your business needs for a full year. You pay one predictable annual fee and receive a dedicated team handling your audit, accounting, VAT, corporate tax, and/or legal advisory — depending on your chosen package. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices.

The UAE Corporate Tax filing deadline for most businesses is September 30, 2026. Businesses that fail to register, file, or pay on time face penalties from the Federal Tax Authority — AED 10,000 for late registration and AED 500 per month for late filing. IHG is accepting CT engagements until August 31, 2026 to ensure full compliance before the deadline.

Yes. IHG is a fully registered UAE tax agency, authorised to represent clients before the Federal Tax Authority for VAT and Corporate Tax matters.

IHG Essentials is suited for early-stage businesses needing foundational compliance — bookkeeping, VAT, and Corporate Tax. IHG Shield is our most popular option for growing SMEs that also need an external audit and FTA support. IHG Pinnacle is designed for established businesses requiring legal advisory, transfer pricing, and complete financial management. Book a free call and we'll recommend the right fit.

Standard onboarding takes 48–72 hours after signing and document submission. For urgent Corporate Tax cases with imminent deadlines, we offer expedited onboarding within 48 hours.

IHG currently serves businesses across Dubai and Abu Dhabi. We work with UAE-based entities regardless of their jurisdiction — mainland, free zone, or offshore structures.

The CT Rescue Pack is a dedicated fast-track service for businesses that have not yet registered for or filed their UAE Corporate Tax return. It covers CT registration, return preparation, filing on EmaraTax, and FTA correspondence — delivered on an accelerated timeline. Intake closes August 31, 2026.
